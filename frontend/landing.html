How it works

From dispute to decision in under a second

No overnight batch jobs, no fragile prompt chains. A deterministic state machine drives each case through a pipeline of specialised executors.

01

Ingest any reason code

Webhooks for card, UPI and wallet networks. The moment a dispute lands, a case is opened with a full audit trail from frame zero.

02

Classify the claim

A TF-IDF embedding maps free-text reason codes to dispute classes — fraud, non-receipt, service. Fast enough to act in milliseconds.

03

Gather the evidence

Dedicated executors pull delivery logs, 3-D Secure results, device and CRM history in parallel — then rate how strong each piece is.

04

Fight or accept, honestly

A score decides whether we contest or recommend acceptance. When confidence clears the threshold, a bank-compliant rebuttal is filed automatically.
